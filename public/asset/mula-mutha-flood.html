Methodology

1 · Reach geometry

The river polygon is traced from the uploaded KML. A centerline is extracted and resampled into — chainage cells over —, which becomes the x-axis for every longitudinal product.

2 · Bed inference

Bed elevation is inferred from width–depth hydraulics along the centerline and reported with a ±σ shading. Bed is never observed directly, so it is always drawn as an uncertainty ribbon rather than a line.

3 · Ensemble forecast

— are propagated over the horizon from the upstream discharge boundary. The median is plotted; P10–P90 is drawn as confidence fog that widens downstream and with lead time.

4 · Thresholds and margin

Each protected asset carries a threshold elevation. Margin is threshold − WSE(now). The margin board shows margin, not a bare stage value, because margin is what an operator acts on.

5 · Exceedance probability

P(exceed 72 h) is the fraction of ensemble members crossing the threshold within the horizon. Time-to-threshold is the first crossing of the ensemble median, so it can be absent even when P is high.

6 · Scoring

Skill is hindcast-scored on held-out periods: WSE RMSE at 24 h plus hits, misses and false alarms against observed threshold crossings. Bad months are kept in — the scorecard is not filtered.

Discharge, WSE, margins, thresholds and alerts all come from the reach digital twin API. Open-Meteo is not used on this page.

================================================================================
CLIMATEEYE - DATA SOURCES AND REFERENCES
================================================================================

Every number, geometry and tile the app displays, where it comes from, and what
may and may not be claimed about it.

Last updated : 2026-08-14
Maintained by: update this file in the SAME change that adds, removes or
               re-points a data source. See section 9.

Provenance labels used in the UI:
  Live · GloFAS   real observation/forecast from a global model
  Live · ECMWF    real weather forecast
  Model           digital twin output (synthetic hydraulics)
  Estimated       derived in this app from other inputs, not measured
  Demo/Synthetic  invented figures, present only to exercise the software

2. PROJECT BACKEND APIs

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  River digital twin (Mula-Mutha reach)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base        : https://riverdigitaltwin-production.up.railway.app/api
Override    : VITE_FLOOD_API_BASE (see src/lib/floodApi.js)
Used in     : src/lib/floodApi.js, src/components/FloodMapOverlay.jsx
UI label    : Model
Endpoints   : /meta  /state  /margins  /alerts  /scorecard
              /forecast/profile?lead=<h>  /hydrograph?cell=<i>  /advance?hours=<h>

REAL data from this API:
  - reach centreline lat[] / lon[]  (derived from the KML in 3.1)
  - reach_km, n_cells
  - real_width_m[]  per-cell channel width measured off the KML polygon

SYNTHETIC data from this API - demonstration only:
  - q_now discharge (shown as "Discharge" in FloodMapOverlay)
  - water surface elevation, bed level, embankment crest
  - asset thresholds, freeboard margins, exceedance probabilities
  - alerts, scorecard, hydrographs
  - the simulation clock advanced by /advance

Discharge in the flood panel is this twin q_now only. Open-Meteo / GloFAS is
not used (see 1.1 REMOVED).

The API self-labels assets "<Place> - <type> (illustrative)". floodApi.js
strips that suffix for display via cleanLabel()/shortName(), so the caveat
lives in the methodology text instead of on every label.

Do not merge the twin's simulation hour with the GloFAS calendar dates. They
are separate time axes and the UI keeps them separate on purpose.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Figures used:

  highest flood on record       3211 m3/s   (1958)
  record length                 68 years
  severe flood years cited      1944, 1958, 1961, 1976, 1983, 1997, 2005
  post-1961 trend               declining peak magnitudes, rising variability,
                                attributed to Temghar and Varasgaon coming online
  PMC operational target        hold Khadakwasla release below 50,000 cusecs
                                (~1416 m3/s) during the monsoon

Consumed by : src/lib/floodZones.js -> GAUGE_RECORD
Purpose     : the ONLY input to the Q10/Q25/Q100 fit (see 6.1). Since the
              Open-Meteo GloFAS feed was removed, these published figures are
              no longer a bias correction on a model series - they are the
              series. Only the mean annual peak, the record peak and the record
              length are used; there is no year-by-year data behind them.

Primary source: "Disaster Management & Flood Control - An Initiative by Pune
                Municipal Corporation", PMC presentation.
Copy consulted: https://www.slideserve.com/bethanyc/disaster-management-amp-flood-control-an-initiative-by-pune-municipal-corporation-powerpoint-ppt-presentation
CONFIDENCE  : the figures are quoted consistently, but the copy consulted is a
              third-party mirror of the slides. Obtain the original PMC PDF or
              the CWC/WRD gauge record before treating 1165 and 3211 as
              authoritative. Substituting a verified series would change
              Q10/Q25/Q100 directly.

6. DERIVED DATA - COMPUTED IN THIS APP
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Return-period discharge Q10 / Q25 / Q100  ** RESTORED 2026-08-17, OFFLINE **
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Computed in : src/lib/floodZones.js -> getReturnPeriods()
Input       : section 5.1 only - mean annual peak 1165 m3/s, record peak
              3211 m3/s (1958), record length 68 years. No API call; the fit is
              a constant, evaluated in the browser.
Method      : Gumbel (EV1). The record peak is placed at the (n+1)=69-year
              return period using the Weibull plotting position, which with the
              mean gives the two parameters:
                beta = (3211 - 1165) / (y69 - 0.5772) = 560.6
                mu   = 1165 - 0.5772 * beta          = 841.4
              Q(T) = mu + beta * (-ln(-ln(1 - 1/T)))
Current output (m3/s, rounded):
                Q2   1047   (bankfull reference)
                Q5   1682
                Q10  2103
                Q25  2635
                Q50  3029
                Q100 3420
Cross-checks: Q100 3420 sits just above the 1958 record 3211, i.e. that flood
              prices as roughly a 1-in-69 event - consistent by construction.
              Q10 2103 is below the Khadakwasla spillway capacity ~2775 (5.2)
              and well above the PMC monsoon release target ~1416 (5.1).
Caveats     : two published summary statistics, not a gauge series, so there is
              no way to test the Gumbel assumption, no confidence interval, and
              no trend handling - 5.1 itself notes peaks have declined since
              Temghar and Varasgaon came online. The pre-2026-08-14 version of
              these numbers came from GloFAS reanalysis bias-corrected to the
              same publication and read Q10 2103 / Q25 2635 / Q100 3420 after
              correction; the values are close, but this is now literature-only.
UI label    : Estimated

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.2  Map corridors from Q10 / Q25 / Q100  ** RESTORED 2026-08-17 **
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Computed in : src/lib/floodZones.js -> buildFloodZones()
Inputs      : 6.1 discharges, plus the per-cell channel width real_width_m from
              the twin /meta (3.1 KML geometry), smoothed over 25 cells.
Method      : downstream hydraulic geometry W = a*Q^0.5, bankfull taken as Q2,
              so corridor width = channel width * (Q_T / Q2)^0.5, capped at
              channel width + 900 m. The width is offset either side of the
              centreline along the local normal to make one polygon per return
              period, ~683 vertices each. Q is uniform along the reach - with a
              single gauge there is no upstream/downstream gradient to apply.
Current output (reach 16.96 km, 1698 cells):
                10-year   mean width 259 m   range 173-567   area 4.4 km2
                25-year   mean width 290 m   range 194-635   area 4.9 km2
                100-year  mean width 330 m   range 221-723   area 5.6 km2
Caveats     : NO DEM and no bathymetry are involved. This is a width scaled off
              a channel outline, so it cannot represent terrain, embankments,
              backwater, culverts or urban drainage, and real inundation is
              patchy rather than a smooth band. Treat the ranking of the three
              zones as far more meaningful than any edge on the map.
Consumed by : src/components/FloodMapOverlay.jsx (checkbox per return period,
              discharge / mean width / area per row) -> Dashboard.jsx ->
              MapComponent.jsx layers lyr-flood-zone-fill / -line, drawn under
              the reach outline.
UI label    : Estimated


================================================================================
7. KNOWN GAPS - DATA WE DO NOT HAVE
================================================================================

Not integrated, and each would materially improve accuracy:

  - Maharashtra WRD official red line / blue line flood polygons for the Pune
    Mula and Mutha segments. This is the correct source for regulatory zones.
  - CWC / Khadakwasla observed daily gauge archive. Would replace the single
    published mean in 5.1 with a real series and remove the reliance on a
    mirrored slide deck.
  - A DEM or bathymetry for the reach, needed before any inundation extent can
    be claimed rather than a corridor width.
  - Real hydraulics for water surface elevation, freeboard and thresholds,
    which are currently synthetic (2.1).
  - CPCB / MPCB observed BOD and COD, to replace the synthetic figures in 3.2.
  - Google Flood Hub API - evaluated, not integrated; needs a server-side proxy.
  - Sentinel-2 or JRC Global Surface Water for observed water extent.
  - Gauge-based validation of the AQI and weather backend (2.2).

Also missing by construction: the return-period fit (6.1) has only two numbers
behind it - a mean and a single record peak - so individual floods such as the
1961 Panshet-Khadakwasla dam failure, 2005 or 2019 are nowhere in it. A real
annual maximum series is the single change that would most improve the flood
zones.


================================================================================
8. HOW TO CITE THE FLOOD NUMBERS
================================================================================

Acceptable:
  "Return periods fitted (Gumbel) to the published Khadakwasla annual peak
   statistics, 1940-2007; estimated corridor, not a surveyed flood line."

Not acceptable:
  "100-year flood zone" presented as a regulatory or planning boundary
  "based on the Pune gauge record"    (two summary figures, not a series - 6.1)
  "matches Pune flood records"        (only 1958 anchors the fit)
  any use for emergency response, land-use decisions or property-level risk
